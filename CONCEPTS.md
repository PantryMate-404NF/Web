# Concepts

Shared domain vocabulary for this project — entities, named processes, and status concepts with project-specific meaning. Seeded with core domain vocabulary, then accretes as ce-compound and ce-compound-refresh process learnings; direct edits are fine. Glossary only, not a spec or catch-all.

## Pantry

### Pantry Item

A food ingredient the user has registered as available at home, with storage and date information that supports pantry views and recipe decisions.

### Receipt Review Draft

The temporary, user-editable result of recognizing a purchase receipt; each ingredient remains a draft until its date, name, and storage choice are confirmed and that ingredient is successfully registered as a pantry item.
